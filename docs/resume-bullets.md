# Resume Bullets - E-Commerce Intelligence System

- **Architected and developed an end-to-end E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Intelligence platform**, processing 100k+ transactions to generate automated business insights, forecasting, and ML-driven segmentation.
- **Designed a robust PostgreSQL data warehouse** and engineered a Python-based ETL pipeline (Pandas/NumPy) to validate, clean, and load complex relational e-commerce data.
- **Developed 40+ advanced SQL analytical queries** to uncover key business drivers, analyzing Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and product profitability.
- **Implemented K-Means clustering for RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation**, categorizing 20k+ customers into actionable cohorts to drive targeted marketing strategies.
- **Built a predictive churn model using Random Forest** (ROC-AUC 0.60+), identifying at-risk customers and calculating churn probability based on historical purchasing behavior.
- **Deployed an ARIMA time-series forecasting model** utilizing `statsmodels` to predict the next 6 months of revenue, providing strategic visibility to stakeholders.
- **Created an interactive React/Vite dashboard** with Tailwind CSS and Recharts, integrated with a FastAPI backend, enabling real-time visualization of KPIs, cohort analysis, and automated business recommendations.
- *(Note: Metrics are based on highly realistic synthesized datasets mirroring actual e-commerce patterns).*
